01 — Profile

About

I'm a Mechanical Engineering graduate from PIEAS with a CGPA of 3.74, focused on CAD design in SolidWorks, mechanical systems simulation, and hands-on maintenance work. My final year was spent designing a tracked rescue robot on screen and then cutting, drilling, and welding its parts on the shop floor — I like engineering that goes both ways, from model to metal and back.

I spent a summer at Fauji Fertilizer Company's Goth Machi plant maintaining rotary and static equipment — valves, heat exchangers, piping, cooling towers, boilers — which is where a lot of the hardware in my design gallery below draws its inspiration from. I'm currently looking for roles in design engineering, manufacturing, or robotics where I can keep working across both sides of that line.
